* **buildLoreContext independent budgets**: \`buildLoreContext()\` reserves output and overhead first, then allocates separate budgets for archived-excluded distillations (25%) and the recent raw tail (40%). Chose independent budgets over one shared pool because the gradient contract keeps distillation from displacing the raw tail; LTM is omitted because the gateway injects it separately, and including it would duplicate context.

Tool-role content remains a string, and the breakpoint walks backward past tool results or tool-call-only messages. Chose stable array-form history over preserving plain strings because the moving breakpoint otherwise flips historical message bytes between turns and destroys provider cache hits.
